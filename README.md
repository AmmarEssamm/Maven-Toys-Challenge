# Maven-Toys-Challenge – Power Bi Project

## Overview

This project was developed as part of the Maven Toys Challenge, where I acted as a Business Intelligence Consultant for Maven Toys, a fictitious toy retail chain operating across Mexico. The goal was to analyze historical sales and inventory data to identify performance trends and support a data-driven store expansion strategy.

## Business Problem

Maven Toys is planning to expand its physical store presence and requires clear, actionable insights to determine:

Which store locations perform best

Which product categories drive the highest demand

When sales and profit peak throughout the year

This analysis supports strategic decisions around where to expand, what to sell, and when to scale.

## Data & Tools

Dataset: Mexico Toy Sales (Maven Analytics)

Data Scope: Sales transactions, products, stores, inventory, revenue, and profit

Tools Used:

Power BI

DAX for KPI creation

Data modeling and time-series analysis

## Dashboard Summary

A single-page executive dashboard was designed to provide a clear and concise view of business performance, featuring:

Key KPIs: Total Orders, Total Revenue, Total Profit

Time-Series Analysis: Monthly revenue and profit trends

Product Performance: Orders by product category

Store Analysis: Performance by store location with interactive filtering

The dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly identify trends, compare performance, and explore insights interactively.

## Key Insights

Toys and Art & Crafts are the highest-performing product categories by order volume.

Revenue and profit exhibit seasonal patterns, indicating optimal timing for new store launches.

Certain store location types consistently outperform others, providing a strong blueprint for expansion.

Overall profitability trends suggest the business is well-positioned for sustainable growth.

## Strategic Recommendation

Based on the analysis, Maven Toys should:

Expand into store locations with proven high performance

Prioritize inventory for top-selling product categories

Time expansion initiatives to align with peak sales periods

These actions reduce risk and maximize return on investment for future store openings.
