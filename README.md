# Maven-Toys-Challenge – Power Bi Project


## Project Overview

This project was created as part of the Maven Toys Challenge, where I assumed the role of a BI Consultant for Maven Toys, a fictitious chain of toy stores operating in Mexico.

The objective was to analyze sales and inventory data to uncover key trends and propose a data-driven expansion strategy supported by a single-page interactive dashboard.

## Business Objective

Identify sales, revenue, and profit trends over time

Analyze product category performance

Evaluate store location performance

Provide actionable insights to support new store expansion decisions

## Dataset

Source: Maven Analytics – Mexico Toy Sales Dataset

Data Includes:

Daily sales transactions

Products & categories

Store locations

Inventory levels

Revenue & profit metrics

## Tools & Technologies

Power BI

DAX (Measures & KPIs)

Data modeling & visualization

Time series analysis

## Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard provides a high-level executive view, including:

KPI Cards

Total Orders

Total Revenue

Total Profit

Monthly Trends

Revenue over time

Profit patterns by month

Product Category Analysis

Orders by product category

Store Location Filter

Compare performance across Airport, Commercial, Downtown, and Residential stores

🔍 Key Insights

Toys and Art & Crafts are the top-performing product categories by total orders.

Revenue shows seasonal spikes, indicating strong demand periods that should be leveraged for expansion.

Certain store locations consistently outperform others, suggesting ideal models for new store placement.

Stable profit trends indicate the business is ready for controlled geographic expansion.
